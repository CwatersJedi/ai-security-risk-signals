# Adversary Adaptation Model

## Premise
When defenders adopt AI tooling, attackers rarely remain static. They adapt to the new environment.

## Adaptation Questions
1. What defensive capability is becoming easier or faster?
2. How visible is that change to attackers?
3. What attacker behavior is most likely to shift first?
4. What weak signal would indicate adaptation is already underway?
5. What second-order effect might matter more than the initial change?

## Common Adaptation Paths
- Evasion of common model-driven patterns
- Noise injection to degrade triage quality
- Social engineering tuned to AI-assisted workflows
- Exploitation of over-trusted automation
- Shift toward less-monitored channels
