# Case Study: The Human Analyst in the Loop

## Key Judgment
AI security tools do not eliminate the need for analysts; they change where judgment is most valuable.

## Analyst Value Shifts Toward
- validating machine-generated conclusions
- identifying model blind spots
- assessing second-order risk
- connecting incidents into broader narratives
- making decisions under uncertainty where data is incomplete

## Strategic Implication
Organizations that treat AI as a substitute for judgment may move faster initially, but they are more exposed to brittle workflows, silent error propagation, and governance failures.
