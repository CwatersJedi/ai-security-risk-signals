# Case Study: Defender Acceleration vs. Adversary Adaptation

## Thesis
Every meaningful defensive acceleration changes the adversary environment. The strategic question is whether the net effect improves resilience faster than attackers can adjust.

## Example Pattern
- Defender adopts AI triage assistant
- Alert review time falls significantly
- Analysts increasingly accept summaries at face value
- Attackers test edge-case behaviors that appear low-severity in summary output
- Security team reports stronger response metrics while subtle compromise risk grows

## Implication
Operational metrics can improve while strategic risk quietly shifts. This is why AI tooling should be assessed as both a defensive capability and a signal of future attacker behavior.
