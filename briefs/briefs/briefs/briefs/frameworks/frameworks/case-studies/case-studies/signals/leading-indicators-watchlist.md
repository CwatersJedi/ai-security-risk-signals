# Leading Indicators Watchlist

Track these indicators as AI becomes more embedded in security operations.

## Capability Indicators
- Growth in AI-assisted alert triage
- Increased use of AI-generated hunt queries
- Wider adoption of AI-based remediation ranking

## Risk Indicators
- Analyst over-trust in model outputs
- Rising automation without equivalent governance updates
- Increased model-driven false confidence
- Vendor opacity around prioritization or reasoning

## Adversary Indicators
- More tailored attacks that exploit known triage assumptions
- Attack behavior designed to blend into summarized low-severity narratives
- More exploitation of under-prioritized or chained exposures
