# Strategic Risk Brief: Security Automation and Over-Reliance Risk

## Key Judgment
AI-enhanced security automation can compress response time and reduce analyst workload, but may also normalize low-friction automated decisions that outpace governance, create hidden failure modes, and erode human understanding of security operations.

## Confidence
Moderate

## Risk Statement
The central strategic risk is not only incorrect action. It is gradual dependency on automation without equivalent investment in decision governance, exception handling, and failure review.

## Indicators to Monitor
- Rising percentage of closed alerts without human review
- Reduced analyst challenge of AI recommendations
- Inconsistent override rates across teams
- Increase in recurring incidents despite faster response metrics

## Recommendations
1. Establish automation review thresholds
2. Track when analysts override AI actions and why
3. Separate speed metrics from decision-quality metrics
4. Conduct periodic failure-mode reviews
