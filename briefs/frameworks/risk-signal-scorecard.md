# Risk Signal Scorecard

Use this scorecard to evaluate an AI security tool as a strategic signal.

## Scoring Dimensions
Rate each category from 1 (low) to 5 (high).

### 1. Defender Acceleration
How much does the tool increase the speed or scale of defensive work?

### 2. Adversary Learning Value
How likely is it that attacker observation of this capability changes adversary behavior?

### 3. Workflow Dependence Risk
How likely are teams to become operationally dependent on the tool?

### 4. Explainability Gap
How difficult is it to understand or audit the tool's reasoning?

### 5. Governance Exposure
How much new risk does the tool create around privacy, overreach, bad automation, or accountability?
